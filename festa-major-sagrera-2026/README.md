# Festa Major de la Sagrera 2026

An unofficial, hour-by-hour guide to the Festa Major de la Sagrera in Barcelona,
November 12–22, 2026.
Available in Catalan, Castilian and English.

## About

A single self-contained HTML page. No build step, no dependencies, no tracking.
Each day has a timeline with times, places, directions and calendar links, plus
links to the official sign-up pages the program names. It is followed by a list
of venues and the practical information from the official program.

## Source

All information on this page was gathered from the
[official program](https://drive.google.com/file/d/14_2hLpFjh14X_8BzqkhWWQmQmNkioD4j/view)
of the Festa Major de la Sagrera 2026, published by the Comissió de Festes de la
Sagrera. Please check the program for the full details and any changes.

This is a personal project, made so that everyone can enjoy the festivities more
easily. It is not an official publication.
